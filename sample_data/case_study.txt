Case Study: Greenfield Bakery Co.

CLIENT
Greenfield Bakery Co., a family-owned bakery chain with 4 locations, wanted to
launch online ordering and grow their local delivery customer base.

CHALLENGE
Their existing website was outdated, not mobile-friendly, and had no way for
customers to place orders online. They were losing customers to competitors
with modern ordering systems.

WHAT WE DID
- Designed and built a new 8-page responsive website with online ordering
- Set up Google Ads campaign targeting local delivery searches
- Ran social media management for 3 months to build local following

RESULTS
- Online orders went from 0 to 340 per month within 90 days
- Website mobile bounce rate dropped from 68% to 24%
- Instagram following grew from 200 to 2,100 followers
- Client renewed their marketing retainer for a full year

CLIENT QUOTE
"They understood exactly what a small local business needs — not a bloated
enterprise site, just something that actually gets us orders."

TIMELINE
6 weeks from kickoff to launch, ongoing marketing retainer since.

Case Study: Voltage Fitness Studio

CLIENT
Voltage Fitness Studio, a boutique gym chain expanding to a second location.

CHALLENGE
Needed a stronger brand identity before opening the second location, plus a
way to track leads from their website into their booking system.

WHAT WE DID
- Full brand identity kit: new logo, color palette, signage-ready brand guide
- Landing page built specifically for the new location's pre-launch signups
- SEO audit ahead of the second location's local search presence

RESULTS
- 480 pre-launch signups before the second location even opened
- New brand identity used across signage, merch, and social consistently
- Ranked on page 1 for "gym near [neighborhood]" within 8 weeks of launch

TIMELINE
3 weeks for brand identity, 1 week for landing page, delivered in parallel.
